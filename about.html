My Story

Built in Harlem, shaped by purpose.

Born and raised in Harlem to immigrant parents from Ivory Coast, I found inspiration through media when positive examples felt out of reach. Movies, sports broadcasts, and even commercials showed me possibility and planted the confidence to create a different future.

That curiosity became discipline. I studied what creates excellence, then translated those lessons into collaborative, cinematic work. My goal is simple: help people and brands tell stories that feel honest, bold, and unforgettable.
